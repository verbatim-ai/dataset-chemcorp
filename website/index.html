What the company does

ChemCorp buys bulk chemical raw materials by the tonne, processes and repackages them, and sells solvents, acids, bases and speciality chemicals by the litre and kilogram to industrial formulators across Western Europe. It runs production at Villeurbanne and Roussillon, ships through the Lyon-Est chemical terminal, and holds a safety stock at Limonest.

The company is certified to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 and runs a COFRAC-accredited laboratory (No. 1-5421). Its stated ambition is to become the leading supplier of bio-based, low-impact chemical specialities in Southern and Western Europe — a transition documented across the strategy papers, R&D minutes and customer meetings in this corpus.

Why this exists

Real corporate corpora cannot be published — they carry commercial and personal data. A synthetic one can, and it can be built to be hard in the ways that matter for retrieval: near-identical boilerplate repeated across documents of the same type, facts that only resolve by combining two sources, deliberately inconsistent batch numbering, and two product catalogues that overlap on exactly two chemicals under different reference codes.

Every document is produced by a committed Python generator, so the corpus is reproducible and every expected answer can be traced to the line of code that emitted it.
