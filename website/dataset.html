Conventions

All content is in English. Amounts are in euros with English number formatting (1,556.40), VAT is a flat 20%, and dates are ISO YYYY-MM-DD in metadata and tables, spelled out in prose. Reference codes keep French-derived prefixes — FC facture, BC bon de commande, FT fiche technique, CRI/CRC compte rendu interne/client — because they are identifiers that benchmark questions cite verbatim.

Business documents span January to June 2024; the analytical datasets cover all twelve months of 2024; strategy documents look ahead to 2027 and 2030.

How the documents connect

There are no foreign keys. Every link between documents is semantic, which is the point:

  • Product reference → data sheet. AK-995 resolves to FT-AK995-2024, and likewise for NaOH-P, H2SO4-96, HCL-33 and PG-USP. Only 5 of the 18 sold products have a sheet.
  • Customer name joins invoices to customer meeting minutes.
  • Supplier name joins purchase orders to internal minutes.
  • KPI values join minutes to strategy papers to datasets — first-pass yield of 94.1% actual against a 97% target, for instance.
  • No invoice cites an order number and no order cites an invoice. Sales and purchasing are separate universes by design.

Batch numbering is intentionally inconsistent across document types, so there is no single registry to resolve against.

Regenerating the corpus

Clone the repository and run ./build.sh. It creates a virtualenv, installs reportlab and runs all seven document generators plus this site. Output lands in website/docs/. Six generators are fully deterministic; the analytical datasets use random.seed(42), so a complete run reproduces identical values.
